Questions

Frequently asked questions

A few things people often ask before booking balloon decor in Schaumburg and nearby towns. If you don’t see your question, get in touch.

How far in advance should I book?

It’s best to get in touch as soon as you have a date, especially for weekends and peak seasons. We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance, though last-minute bookings are sometimes available depending on calendar and inventory openings. You can request a free quote anytime with your event date, location, and the look you have in mind. (Please note: listed prices do not include tax or delivery fees.)

Do you charge for delivery and setup?

Yes. Listed package prices do not include tax or a delivery fee. Delivery is quoted separately based on your event location and distance from Schaumburg, venue access requirements, and any applicable permits or venue fees passed through at cost. Setup is included with backdrop and balloon packages, and late-night pickup after 10 PM incurs an additional $25 fee.

Do you decorate indoors and outdoors?

Yes. Listed package prices are for indoor setups and do not include tax or delivery fees. Outdoor setups are available and incur an additional fee based on extra anchoring and wind-stabilization needs and setup complexity. If your celebration is outside, share the venue details when you request a quote so the design can be planned for the space and weather.

How long does setup take?

Setup time depends on the package and the venue. Typically, smaller setups like the Mini-Event Bundle take around 30–45 minutes, while larger installations like the Showstopper (with a three-panel backdrop and 30 ft garland) take about 1.5–2 hours. We will share a setup window when your booking is confirmed so you can plan access to the space.

What is included in a package?

Backdrop packages include a backdrop, basic vinyl or basic signage, and a balloon garland in the size for that tier: Mini-Event Bundle (one small panel and a 9 ft garland, starts at $280), Standard (one panel and a 14 ft garland, starts at $350), Signature (two-panel backdrop and a 24 ft garland, starts at $475), and Showstopper (three-panel backdrop and a 30 ft garland, starts at $625). Balloon stacks start at $150, and upgraded balloon stacks start at $250. All arrangements are crafted with air-filled, solid-colored latex balloons, and backdrop setups use sturdy 7-foot wooden panels. All listed prices do not include tax, delivery fees, or specialty props (which are available as add-ons), and backdrop and balloon packages have a $280 minimum order before tax and delivery.

How do deposits and payments work?

A 20% non-refundable deposit is required to reserve your date via HoneyBook, with the remaining balance due upon completion of the setup. We accept standard electronic payments through HoneyBook invoicing. All prices are subject to applicable tax and delivery fees. You can start by requesting a free quote.

Do you take the balloons down after the event?

Post-event tear-down is included in all packages except the Mini-Event Bundle. Pickup windows are scheduled in advance with the client, and late-night pickup after 10 PM incurs an additional $25 fee.

What happens in hot weather or rain?

Balloons are sensitive to heat, direct sun, and strong wind, and rain can affect outdoor setups. Because outdoor weather conditions are unpredictable and can affect balloon longevity and structure, outdoor setups are undertaken at the client’s weather risk with no refunds or reschedules solely for weather, so we strongly recommend having a shaded or indoor backup option. If your event is outdoors or the forecast looks difficult, we’ll talk through options such as a shaded or indoor placement.

Can I rent a backdrop without balloons, or keep a backdrop after the party?

We do not rent out backdrops on their own. Customers can keep the backdrop from the Mini-Event Bundle, while keeping backdrops from any other package is not an option.

What if I need to change or cancel my booking?

If your date, venue, or design needs to change, or you need to cancel, please get in touch as soon as you can. Date or design changes are subject to availability (and any applicable rescheduling fee), while the 20% booking deposit is non-refundable once your date is reserved.

Can you match a color palette or theme?

Yes. Each setup is designed around your occasion. Share colors, a theme, or inspiration photos when you request a quote, and Raisa will design a balloon look using solid-colored latex balloons and basic vinyl or signage (with custom props available as add-ons) that fits your celebration.

What does my venue need to provide?

Most setups need clear floor space, a reasonably clean surface for the backdrop, and access to the room at the agreed setup time. Please ensure unobstructed loading and unloading access, sufficient ceiling clearance for tall garlands or panels, and a flat, level surface; backdrops are freestanding, weighted structures and do not rely on attaching to venue walls. If your venue has rules about decor or installation, share them when you book.

What towns do you serve?

Raisa's Balloons is based in Schaumburg, Illinois, and serves Schaumburg and towns within about 20 miles across Cook County and surrounding northwest suburbs, including Palatine, Roselle, Hoffman Estates, Elk Grove Village, Arlington Heights, Bartlett, Niles, Streamwood, and Carol Stream. A full list is on the Contact page. Events a little farther out may still be possible, so just ask when you request a quote; travel beyond 20 miles is considered case by case and carries an additional distance and delivery fee (and prices do not include tax or delivery).
